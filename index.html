Supporting Student Wellness

A digital platform that helps students monitor their wellbeing and connects them with the right support when they need it.

About the System

The Student Wellness & Early Intervention System provides a centralized platform for students, counsellors, faculty members and administrators.

The system uses wellness assessments, support requests and role-based access to help identify students who may require additional support.

System Features

Student Wellness

Complete wellness assessments and monitor your wellbeing over time.

Counselling Support

Connect with counsellors and manage support cases.

Faculty Referral

Faculty can refer students who may require academic or wellness support.

Administration

Administrators can monitor users, cases and system statistics.
